# Research principles

- A win or loss is an observation, not proof of an edge or proof of failure.
- Compare expectancy, win rate, drawdown, costs, uncertainty, sample size, and out-of-sample robustness.
- Preserve failed, skipped, and discretionary opportunities where practical; do not learn only from winners.
- Similar setups may not be statistically independent.
- Candidate models can be compared, combined, retired, and reintroduced with lineage.
- No automatic promotion to live trading.
